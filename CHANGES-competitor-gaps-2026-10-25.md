## New pages (32)

- `/gpa-to-letter-grade/` — GPA → letter grade, A–F and plus/minus scales,
  exact values and between-letter intervals, both conversion tables.
- `/letter-grade-to-gpa/` — letter → grade points on both scales, with
  the points × credits = quality points bridge.
- `/high-school-gpa-calculator/` — weighted + unweighted side by side,
  regular / honors / AP levels, adjustable bonuses. Example computed:
  3.66 unweighted, 4.16 weighted.
- `/college-gpa-calculator/` — credit-hour weighting, semester result
  plus cumulative path. Example computed: 15 credits, 3.60 GPA.
- `/final-grade-planner/` — up to 10 classes: current grade, final weight
  and target per class, required final score per class, with secured /
  possible / impossible verdicts. The Biology example (84%, 25% weight,
  90% target) is honestly flagged impossible at 108%.
- `/cgpa-to-percentage/<university>/` — 25 pages, one per verified
  university/body formula (VTU, Mumbai University, MAKAUT, GTU, SPPU,
  Anna, BHU, BITS Pilani, CBSE, AICTE and 15 more). Each has its own
  converter using that institution's own formula, its source note, three
  computed example conversions and FAQs. Unverified institutions get no
  page; the generic standard formula gets no page.
- `/blog/grading-systems-explained/` — A–F, plus/minus, 7-point,
  pass/fail, credit/no credit, standards-based, mastery, contract,
  specifications and ungrading, with the same scores graded under three
  letter scales (computed) and a comparison table.
- `/blog/high-school-vs-college-gpa/` — the same five grades computed
  three ways (HS unweighted 3.66, HS weighted 4.16, college
  credit-weighted 3.55), plus cumulative-GPA behaviour by year.

## Changed behaviour

- `/gpa-calculator/` now saves courses, the A+ value and prior
  GPA/credits on the device only (localStorage, key
  `egt-gpa-calculator-v1`), restores them on load, and adds a "Clear
  saved courses" button. Nothing is uploaded.
- The high school and college pages use their own tool defaults and
  storage keys (`egt-high-school-gpa-v1`, `egt-college-gpa-v1`), so they
  never read or overwrite the weighted / semester pages' saved rows.
  The letter-to-GPA page opens on the letter direction by default.
- PageSpeed leftovers from the 2026-10-24 pack (never confirmed
  uploaded): the stale-asset recovery script is now inlined in
  `index.html` instead of a separate render-blocking request, and
  tool-chunk warm-up moved from 2.5 s to 6 s after load.
- `CONTENT_REVIEWED_ON` bumped to 2026-10-25 (visible "last updated"
  dates on ToolPages).
- Footer now links every new page (the two blog posts plus all 25
  university pages, generated from the same data as the pages), so every
  sitemap URL stays one click from the homepage, as the launch audit
  requires.
- `scripts/generate_og_cards.py` now covers the new pages, and adds one
  card per verified university automatically (loop over
  `cgpaUniversities.ts`). Run `python3 scripts/generate_og_cards.py
  --missing` after any future university addition.
