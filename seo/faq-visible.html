Grade calculator FAQ

How do I calculate a test grade from the number of questions?

Divide the number of correct answers by the total number of questions and multiply by 100. For example, 17 correct out of 20 is 17 ÷ 20 × 100 = 85%.

How do I print a grading chart for my class?

Enter the number of questions, then press Print chart. The page prints only the grading table, without the site menu or extra text.

Can I use this grade calculator offline?

Yes. After your first visit the calculator is saved on your device and keeps working without an internet connection.

Can I change the A, B, C and D cut-offs?

Yes. Open Advanced settings and set your own percentage cut-offs for each letter grade. The chart updates immediately.

What is the difference between a number-correct chart and a number-missed chart?

A number-correct chart lists scores from the top down by questions answered correctly. A number-missed chart lists the percent for each count of questions a student got wrong, which is faster when you mark errors on papers.

Is my data stored on a server?

No. All calculations run in your browser. Your settings are saved only on your own device.
