# Social Share Cards (1200x630px)

This directory contains Open Graph and Twitter Card social preview images (1200x630px, aspect ratio 1.91:1) referenced in `src/components/SEO.tsx` and `src/data/seoConfig.ts`.

## How to Swap In Your Custom Images
Simply replace any of these PNG image files with your designed 1200x630px cards:

1. `grade-calculator-1200x630.png` - Used by the Free Grade Calculator (weighted & final exam)
2. `gpa-calculator-1200x630.png` - Used by the Free Online GPA Calculator
3. `cgpa-calculator-1200x630.png` - Used by the Free CGPA to Percentage Calculator
4. `tip-calculator-1200x630.png` - Used by the Free Online Tip & Bill Splitter
5. `percentage-calculator-1200x630.png` - Used by the Free Online Percentage Calculator
6. `loan-calculator-1200x630.png` - Used by the Free Online Loan Calculator
7. `mortgage-calculator-1200x630.png` - Used by the Free Online Mortgage Calculator
8. `password-generator-1200x630.png` - Used by the Free Online Strong Password Generator
